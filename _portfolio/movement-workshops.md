Since 2024, I design spaces where adults can enjoy playfulness in groups.
My participatory performance and public space interventions practice include improvisation theater, movement, physical theater, dance and participatory facilitation. 
Through Playing Silly and Queering Improv, I create between 90-minute and 2 hours sessions where people, especially marginalized folx, can reconnect with their bodies, their weirdness, and each other.

I'm particularly interested in what happens when queer feminist values shape the structure of play itself: who gets to lead, who gets to take up space, what counts as a valid contribution.
